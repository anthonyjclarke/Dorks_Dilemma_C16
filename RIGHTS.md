# Rights and Preservation

## Historical Software

The surviving Dork's Dilemma source, under its development name Rooms of Peril, credits Micro-Projects Ltd. and Gremlin Graphics Ltd., with 1985 notices. Those notices are still in the preserved source. The game binary and the historical source keep whatever rights status they already had.

---

## Printed Material and Artwork

The cover art, cassette packaging, scrapbook pages, reviews, logos and artwork here stay with their respective rights holders. The [Artifact Catalogue](docs/ARTIFACTS.md) lists what I've included.

---

## Repository Status

I've set this repository up for studying, reassembling and preserving the historical material. I haven't assigned any new open-source or artwork licence. Making the repository public doesn't grant any rights beyond a licence that's stated explicitly.

This is the first of my Micro Projects restorations, and its build approach is reused in my Tycoon Tex, Xargon Wars and Xargon's Revenge repositories. The build never rewrites the original input files.
